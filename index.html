Features

One-Tap Open

Tap a door to unlock it instantly. Star your favorites for quick access from the main screen.

Routes

Chain multiple doors into a route and open them all in sequence with a single tap.

Widgets & Control Center

Open your favorite doors from the home screen widget or the iOS Control Center — no need to launch the app.

Apple Watch

Open doors straight from your wrist. The Watch app shows your starred doors for instant access.

Guest Codes

Generate temporary access codes for visitors. Set expiry times and revoke access anytime.

Face ID Security

Lock the app behind Face ID. Your credentials never leave the device — stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

How it works

1

Configure your system

Enter your RCO M5 server host, server ID, and system name in the app settings.

2

Log in with your building credentials

Use the username and password provided by your building administrator to authenticate.

3

Star your favorite doors

Browse the door list and star the ones you use most. They'll appear at the top for quick access.

4

Open doors from anywhere

Use the app, home screen widget, Control Center, or Apple Watch — whichever is fastest.
