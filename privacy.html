Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Portly collects no data about you. Your credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. The app communicates only with your own RCO M5 server over HTTPS; door names you rename yourself sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services.

Who we are

Portly is an iOS app developed by Karl Wallbom. Open an issue at github.com/kalleeh/portly/issues.

What data we collect

We collect nothing. Portly does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data with us or any third party. Apart from requests to your RCO M5 server and your own device backups, the only app data that leaves your device is your custom door names, which iOS syncs through your own iCloud account (see below).

What happens on your device

The app stores the following data on your device:

DataWhere storedPurpose
Credentials (username & password)iOS Keychain (device-only, no iCloud sync)Authenticates with your RCO M5 server
System configuration (host, server ID, system name)iOS Keychain (device-only, no iCloud sync)Connects to the correct RCO M5 server
Access tokeniOS Keychain (device-only, no iCloud sync)Maintains authenticated session
Door favoritesUserDefaults (on-device)Shows starred doors for quick access
RoutesUserDefaults (on-device)Stores door sequences for one-tap route opening
Custom door namesUserDefaults (on-device) and iCloud key-value storageKeeps the names you give doors in sync across your devices

Keychain items are device-only: they are never synced to iCloud and are not restored to another device from a backup. App data stored in UserDefaults (favorites, routes, door names) may be included in your regular iCloud or computer device backups, like any app's data. Custom door names (door ID, the name you chose and when you changed it) are also stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage so they appear on your other devices; credentials and tokens are never stored there. None of this data is shared with us.

Network connections

Portly makes these outbound connections:

  • HTTPS requests — to your RCO M5 server only, using the host you configured. These carry authentication and door-open commands.
  • iCloud key-value storage — handled by iOS through your Apple Account, carrying only your custom door names (see above). Apple's own privacy policy applies to iCloud.

No other connections are made to third-party servers. There are no analytics endpoints, no crash reporting services, and no telemetry of any kind.

Biometrics (Face ID)

If you enable Face ID, the app uses the iOS LocalAuthentication framework to verify your identity before unlocking. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and never leaves the Secure Enclave on your device. Portly never accesses, stores, or transmits biometric data.

Camera

Portly does not use the camera.

Analytics and crash reporting

There are none. Portly contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no telemetry of any kind.

Children's privacy

Portly is a utility app not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because we collect no data, changes are unlikely.
